# ORSAVEN — Visual Identity Direction

## Desired identity

Premium, elegant, modern, restrained, clean, mature, and timeless. The identity should feel credible for both downloadable products and higher-value implementation work.

## Design principles

- Strong wordmark first; symbol is optional and must add meaning.
- Prefer a distinctive, legible typographic treatment over decorative complexity.
- Use generous whitespace and a disciplined grid.
- Keep layouts calm and editorial, with clear hierarchy.
- Use product screenshots and real artifacts rather than generic AI imagery.
- Ensure contrast, readability, and responsive behavior.

## Initial visual territory

A restrained neutral foundation (warm white, charcoal, and a muted stone tone) with one considered accent color. Final color values and typography must be selected through visual exploration and accessibility checks—not treated as approved specifications in this document.

## Logo requirements

- Primary horizontal wordmark.
- Compact symbol or monogram only if it remains recognizable at small sizes.
- Light and dark variants.
- SVG master and optimized web formats.
- Clear-space, minimum-size, and misuse guidance.
- No similarity-driven imitation of existing venture logos.

## Avoid

Overused AI gradients, glowing brains, robot heads, generic circuit patterns, excessive metallic effects, ornate luxury flourishes, and illegible custom lettering.

## Pending design decisions

Exact logo, symbol, typefaces, color tokens, imagery system, and motion language require a separate visual design review and owner approval.
