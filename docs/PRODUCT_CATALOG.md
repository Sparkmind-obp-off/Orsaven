# ORSAVEN — Product Catalog Architecture

## Catalog taxonomy

### 1. Digital Assets
Downloadable or duplicable resources that help customers create, organize, communicate, or operate.

Examples: templates, playbooks, prompt packs, documents, design resources, spreadsheets, checklists, and workflow blueprints.

Required listing fields: use case, file formats, included files, compatibility, license, update policy, and delivery method.

### 2. Business & System Kits
Structured bundles that help a customer establish or improve a repeatable business or operational process.

Examples: operating kits, SOP collections, launch kits, business workflow systems, and role-specific toolkits.

Required listing fields: target user, problem, setup steps, components, prerequisites, exclusions, maintenance needs, and support level.

### 3. Agentic Solutions
Defined agent workflows, agent configurations, integrations, or orchestration packages.

Examples: task agents, workflow automations, knowledge assistants, and multi-agent process blueprints.

Required listing fields: supported providers, model/tool dependencies, permissions, data handling, human approval points, failure modes, usage costs, setup requirements, and fallback procedure.

Do not imply that ORSAVEN owns or trains the underlying model unless that is factually true and documented.

### 4. Implementation Services
Time-bounded, scoped services to configure, adapt, integrate, or deploy a product.

Required listing fields: scope, deliverables, customer responsibilities, prerequisites, timeline estimate, revision limits, acceptance criteria, exclusions, price, and change-request process.

## Product maturity labels

- Concept: not available for purchase.
- Prototype: experimental; not for production reliance.
- Beta: limited release with stated constraints.
- Available: delivery process tested and support defined.
- Retired: no longer sold; support and migration policy stated.

Only mark a product Available after its delivery checklist and quality review pass.

## Product page minimum standard

1. Product name and category.
2. One-sentence outcome.
3. Intended customer and use case.
4. What is included.
5. What is not included.
6. Screenshots, demo, or sample deliverable where relevant.
7. Requirements and compatibility.
8. Delivery method and expected timing.
9. License, usage rights, and refund policy.
10. Support and update policy.
11. Price and any recurring cost.
12. Version and last-updated date.

## Catalog governance

Every product must have an owner, source/provenance, version, license review, quality checklist, support owner, and retirement plan. Avoid duplicate listings and unclear product ownership.
