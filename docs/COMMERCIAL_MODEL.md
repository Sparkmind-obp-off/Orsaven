# ORSAVEN — Commercial Model

## Model

A curated, product-led catalog with optional implementation services. Start with products that can be delivered reliably and at low marginal cost. Add broader marketplace mechanics only after demand, supply quality, rights, and support capacity are proven.

## Revenue streams

- One-time digital product sales.
- Bundled business and system kits.
- Licensed or subscription access only where ongoing value and support justify recurring billing.
- Fixed-scope implementation services.
- Carefully defined customization or maintenance retainers where operationally sustainable.

Do not introduce recurring billing by default. Make renewal, cancellation, and ongoing obligations explicit.

## Pricing principles

- Price according to customer value, scope, quality, delivery effort, and support burden.
- Show currency, taxes/fees where applicable, and whether a price is one-time or recurring.
- Separate product price from optional implementation.
- Do not promise revenue, savings, or performance without evidence.
- Include a contribution-margin estimate before launching each paid offer.

## Distribution

Potential distribution channels include ORSAVEN's own website and selected third-party storefronts. Channels are not sub-brands and should not dictate ORSAVEN's identity.

Before activating a channel, review its fees, supported countries, payout rules, refund process, content restrictions, and commercial license terms.

## Launch constraints

- Initial build should use free tiers and existing resources where practical.
- No paid subscription, API credit purchase, advertising, or infrastructure upgrade without explicit approval.
- Do not collect customer payments until checkout, tax/consumer disclosures, refund handling, delivery, and support are tested.
- Record product costs and provider dependencies before setting final prices.
