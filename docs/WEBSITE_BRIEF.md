# ORSAVEN — Website Brief

## Purpose

Build a clear, lightweight storefront and product discovery experience for ORSAVEN at https://orsaven.biz.id.

## Primary audiences

Individuals, creators, small-business operators, teams, and organizations looking for practical digital products or implementation assistance.

## Primary user journeys

1. Understand what ORSAVEN offers.
2. Browse by catalog category.
3. Open a product detail page and understand fit, scope, requirements, and price.
4. Purchase or submit an implementation inquiry when the offer is genuinely available.
5. Receive clear delivery and support instructions.

## Suggested information architecture

- Home
- Catalog
  - Digital Assets
  - Business & System Kits
  - Agentic Solutions
  - Implementation Services
- Product detail
- How it works
- About ORSAVEN
- FAQ
- Contact / Support
- Legal: Terms, Privacy, Refund & License

## Homepage sections

1. Hero with concise positioning and catalog CTA.
2. Four catalog categories.
3. Featured products (only verified available products).
4. How ORSAVEN works.
5. Trust and transparency principles.
6. About / ecosystem relationship, without confusing ORSAVEN with source ventures.
7. FAQ and support CTA.
8. Footer with legal and contact links.

## Functional requirements

- Responsive, accessible, fast, and SEO-friendly.
- Clear category navigation and search/filter when catalog size justifies it.
- Product status, version, and pricing visible.
- Contact path that does not expose private operational data.
- No fabricated inventory, testimonials, ratings, or purchase counters.
- Analytics only with appropriate privacy disclosure and consent where required.

## Launch safety

Do not activate paid checkout, customer accounts, email campaigns, analytics subscriptions, or paid infrastructure without explicit approval. Keep draft products clearly unpublished. Legal pages must be reviewed before public commerce.
