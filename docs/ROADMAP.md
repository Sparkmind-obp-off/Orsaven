# ORSAVEN — Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- [x] Brand name selected and locked.
- [x] Productization role and four catalog pillars defined.
- [x] Initial brand and operating documentation.
- [ ] Confirm domain DNS and ownership configuration.
- [ ] Review repository, hosting, and current infrastructure.
- [ ] Approve visual identity direction.

## Phase 1 — Product intelligence
- [ ] Create internal product register.
- [ ] Identify candidate products from existing ventures.
- [ ] Record provenance, ownership, license, dependencies, and delivery costs.
- [ ] Validate customer problems and demand.
- [ ] Select a small pilot catalog.

## Phase 2 — Website foundation
- [ ] Finalize visual identity and design system.
- [ ] Build responsive public pages and category structure.
- [ ] Add product detail templates and maturity labels.
- [ ] Add contact and support flows.
- [ ] Complete accessibility, SEO, security, and performance checks.

## Phase 3 — Controlled pilot
- [ ] Publish only products that pass quality gates.
- [ ] Test delivery and support with a limited audience.
- [ ] Measure conversion, defects, refunds, and support load.
- [ ] Improve product descriptions and onboarding.

## Phase 4 — Commercial readiness
- [ ] Review legal, tax, privacy, consumer, licensing, and payment requirements.
- [ ] Compare distribution channels and payment providers.
- [ ] Obtain explicit approval before enabling paid services.
- [ ] Define launch criteria and incident response.

## Phase 5 — Scale carefully
- [ ] Expand catalog based on proven demand.
- [ ] Add recurring products only when justified.
- [ ] Consider partner/seller marketplace only after governance is designed.
- [ ] Establish regular quality and profitability reviews.

## Non-goals for the initial phase

- Building proprietary foundation models.
- Claiming ownership of third-party AI infrastructure.
- Launching a multi-vendor marketplace before supply and governance are ready.
- Spending money or enabling paid services without approval.
